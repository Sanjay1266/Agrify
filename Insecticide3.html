Safety Information

Your safety is our priority. Follow these guidelines when handling our products.

Handling Precautions

  • Always wear protective equipment when handling products
  • Avoid direct contact with skin, eyes, and respiratory system
  • Keep products away from children and pets
  • Wash hands thoroughly after handling products
  • Never eat, drink, or smoke while handling products

First Aid Measures

  • If ingested, do not induce vomiting and seek medical attention
  • If on skin, wash thoroughly with soap and water
  • If in eyes, rinse continuously with water for 15 minutes
  • If inhaled, move to fresh air and seek medical attention
  • Always bring product label when seeking medical attention

Environmental Safety

  • Avoid application near water sources and drainage systems
  • Dispose of containers according to local regulations
  • Follow recommended application rates to minimize environmental impact
  • Do not apply during flowering periods to protect pollinators
  • Store products in designated areas away from feed and foodstuffs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our insecticide products

How long do your insecticides remain effective after application?

The effectiveness duration varies by product. Our standard formulations typically remain active for 2-4 weeks, while our premium products can provide protection for up to 8 weeks. Weather conditions, particularly rain and intense sunlight, can reduce the effective period. Always check the product label for specific information.

Are your products safe for use around beneficial insects like bees?

We offer several bee-friendly options in our organic and selective insecticide ranges. These products target specific pest species while minimizing impact on beneficial insects. However, as a precaution, we recommend avoiding application during flowering periods when bees are most active. Our product labels clearly indicate bee safety ratings.

What's the difference between systemic and contact insecticides?

Contact insecticides work by directly touching the pest, providing immediate control but requiring thorough coverage. Systemic insecticides are absorbed by the plant and transported throughout its tissues, protecting areas not directly sprayed and offering longer protection. Systemic products are particularly effective against sap-feeding insects but typically require more time to become fully effective.

How soon after application can I harvest edible crops?

The pre-harvest interval (PHI) varies by product and crop. Our organic options typically have PHIs of 1-3 days, while conventional products range from 7-21 days. This information is clearly stated on each product label. Always adhere to these intervals to ensure your produce is safe for consumption.
