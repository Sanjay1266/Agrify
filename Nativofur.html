Premium Fungicide for Superior Disease Control

About Product

  • Nativo is a premium dual-action fungicide with preventive and curative properties
  • Contains Trifloxystrobin 25% + Tebuconazole 50% WG as active ingredients
  • Effectively controls a wide spectrum of fungal diseases in various crops
  • Provides long-lasting protection for up to 14-21 days
  • Enhances crop vigor and productivity through physiological effects

Technical Details

  • Active Ingredients: Trifloxystrobin 25% + Tebuconazole 50% WG
  • Formulation: Water Dispersible Granules (WG)
  • Mode of Action: Combination of QoI (Group 11) and DMI (Group 3) fungicides
  • Shelf Life: 2 years from date of manufacture when stored properly
  • PHI (Pre-Harvest Interval): 15-30 days depending on crop
  • REI (Re-Entry Interval): 24 hours after application

Features

  • Dual action with both protective and curative properties against fungal diseases
  • Mesostemic and systemic movement ensures complete protection of treated plants
  • Resistance management tool due to combination of two different modes of action
  • Rain-fast within 2-3 hours of application for reliable performance
  • Improves photosynthetic efficiency and nitrogen utilization in crops
  • Greening effect enhances chlorophyll content for healthier, more productive plants

Additional Information

  • Application Rate: 0.5-0.75 g per liter of water depending on crop and disease pressure
  • Best applied as preventive treatment before disease onset or at first signs of infection
  • Compatible with most commonly used pesticides and fertilizers in tank mix
  • Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat
  • For optimal results, ensure thorough coverage of plant foliage during application
  • Follow Fungicide Resistance Action Committee (FRAC) guidelines to prevent resistance development
  • Use of spreader-sticker adjuvant recommended for improved adhesion and coverage
