Dual Action Broad-Spectrum Insecticide

About Product

  • Alika is a broad-spectrum insecticide from Syngenta containing Thiamethoxam 12.6% + Lambda-cyhalothrin 9.5% ZC
  • It combines the rapid knockdown action of Lambda-cyhalothrin with the systemic activity of Thiamethoxam
  • Effectively controls a wide range of sucking and chewing pests including aphids, whiteflies, thrips, jassids, and bollworms
  • Provides long-lasting protection for up to 15 days, reducing the need for repeated applications

Technical Details

  • Active Ingredients: Thiamethoxam 12.6% + Lambda-cyhalothrin 9.5% ZC
  • Formulation: Zeon Capsule Suspension (ZC)
  • Mode of Action: Systemic and contact action
  • Shelf Life: 2 years from manufacturing date when stored in cool, dry conditions
  • Application Rate: 10-15 ml per 15 liters of water (varies by crop and pest pressure)
  • PHI (Pre-Harvest Interval): 7-14 days depending on crop

Features

  • Dual-action formula provides both contact and systemic protection
  • Quick knockdown effect on target pests
  • Rainfast within 1 hour of application
  • Compatible with most commonly used pesticides and fertilizers
  • Low odor formula for improved applicator comfort
  • Excellent photostability for longer field performance

Additional Information

  • Wear proper protective equipment during mixing and application
  • Pre-harvest interval: 7-14 days depending on crop (refer to product label)
  • Avoid spraying during hot hours of the day or when rain is expected within 4-6 hours
  • For best results, apply when pest populations are below threshold levels
  • Follow integrated pest management practices for sustainable pest control
