Banana Cultivation

Musa acuminata · Heavy feeder crop with high commercial return
Soil pH
6.5 - 7.5
Ideal Temp
25°C - 32°C
Crop Cycle
11 - 13 Months

Critical Growth Stages & Nutrition

Stage 1

Sucker Planting & Rooting (0-60 Days)

Select disease-free sword suckers weighing 1.5 - 2 kg. Dip in Carbendazim solution before planting. Apply Humic Acid to foster vigorous root network establishment.

Stage 2

Vegetative Canopy Phase (60-180 Days)

Rapid leaf emergence and pseudo-stem thickening. Supply Nitrogen and Potash in split fertigation rounds. Weed control using selective herbicides.

Stage 3

Bunch Shooting & Sizing (180-330 Days)

Inflorescence emerges. Remove male buds (denavelling) and spray micronutrient yield boosters (SOP / Agroveer Banana) for plump, uniform fingers.

Pest & Disease Diagnostic Guide

Sigatoka Leaf Spot Fungal

Yellowish-brown linear spots on leaves that expand into large necrotic patches, leading to premature leaf death and reduced bunch size.

Recommended Remedy: Apply Nativo Fungicide or Amistar Top at 1 ml/L.
Panama Wilt (Fusarium) Soil-Borne

Yellowing of lower leaves progressing upwards, pseudo-stem longitudinal splitting, and reddish-brown discoloration in the vascular ring.

Recommended Remedy: Soil drenching with Bavistin or copper oxychloride around the base.

Turmeric Cultivation

Curcuma longa · High-value rhizomatous spice with curcumin richness
Soil pH
6.0 - 7.5
Ideal Temp
20°C - 35°C
Crop Cycle
8 - 9 Months

Growth Timeline

Stage 1

Rhizome Sprouting (0-45 Days)

Treat mother rhizomes with Mancozeb before planting on raised ridges. Mulch immediately with green leaves to preserve moisture.

Stage 2

Tillering & Leaf Development (45-120 Days)

Application of secondary earthing up. Regular fertigation of NPK 19:19:19 to induce prolific side tillers.

Stage 3

Rhizome Maturation (120-240 Days)

Heavy potassium requirement. Foliar spray of Potash-S and biostimulants to enhance curcumin content and root weight.

Disease Diagnostic & Cure

Rhizome Rot (Pythium) Severe

Collapsing of basal shoots, foul smell, and water-soaked rotting of underground rhizomes during waterlogging.

Recommended Remedy: Drench soil with Ridomil Gold (2.5 g/L) or Blue Copper.
Leaf Spot (Colletotrichum) Foliar

Elliptical brown spots with concentric rings on upper leaves, leading to dried leaf margins.

Recommended Remedy: Foliar spray of Saaf Fungicide at 2 g/L of water.

Tomato Cultivation

Solanum lycopersicum · Popular nightshade crop requiring proactive pest scouting
Soil pH
6.0 - 7.0
Transplant
25 Days Old
Crop Cycle
120 - 150 Days

Growth Timeline

Stage 1

Nursery & Staking (0-35 Days)

Raise seedlings in pro-trays with coco peat. Transplant into well-ploughed beds and install bamboo trellising.

Stage 2

Flowering & Fruit Set (35-75 Days)

Crucial stage for calcium-boron nutrition to prevent blossom end rot. Spray biostimulants like Planofix for blossom retention.

Stage 3

Harvest Flush (75-140 Days)

Multiple pickings of firm, glossy red fruits. Keep soil evenly moist to avoid fruit cracking.

Disease Diagnostic & Cure

Early & Late Blight Fungal

Dark, concentric ring spots on leaves and oily lesions on fruits that rot rapidly in humid monsoon weather.

Recommended Remedy: Spray Kavach or Cabrio Top at 2 g/L.
Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa) Insect Pest

Caterpillars bore clean round entry holes into green and ripening fruits, consuming inner pulp.

Recommended Remedy: Spray Coragen Insecticide (0.4 ml/L) or Exponus.

Rice / Paddy Cultivation

Oryza sativa · India's staple cereal crop requiring water & nitrogen balance
Soil
Clayey Loam
Seed Rate
20 kg / Acre
Duration
110 - 145 Days

Critical Crop Stages

Stage 1

Puddling & Tillering (0-40 Days)

Puddle field thoroughly. Apply basal DAP and zinc sulphate. Maintain 2-3 cm shallow water level during tillering.

Stage 2

Panicle Emergence (40-80 Days)

Flag leaf emergence and panicle development. High potassium requirement (NPK 00:00:50) for maximum grain count.

Stage 3

Milky & Dough Stage (80-120 Days)

Drain water 10 days before harvest when 85% of panicles turn golden yellow.

Pest Diagnostic & Cure

Yellow Stem Borer Larval Pest

Dead heart in vegetative stage and white earheads at heading stage that pull out effortlessly.

Recommended Remedy: Apply Coragen Insecticide or Phoskill.
Paddy Blast (Magnaporthe) Severe

Spindle-shaped lesions with brown borders and grey centers on leaves, nodal rot, and broken panicle necks.

Recommended Remedy: Spray Nativo Fungicide or Bavistin.

Cotton Cultivation

Gossypium hirsutum · White gold cash crop with integrated bollworm management
Soil
Deep Black Soil
Spacing
90 × 60 cm
Duration
150 - 180 Days

Disease Diagnostic & Cure

Pink Bollworm & American Bollworm Severe

Rosetted flowers, shedding of squares, and bore holes in developing cotton bolls stained with frass.

Recommended Remedy: Spray Ampligo Insecticide or Solomon at 1 ml/L.
Whitefly & Sucking Pests Vector

Yellowing, crinkling leaves with sooty mold growth due to honeydew secretions, transmitting leaf curl virus.

Recommended Remedy: Spray Pegasus or Actara.

Cauliflower Cultivation

Brassica oleracea var. botrytis · Curd formation and cole crop protection

Pests & Diagnostics

Diamondback Moth (DBM) Pest

Larvae feed on undersides of leaves, producing windowing holes and rendering curds unmarketable.

Recommended Remedy: Spray Exponus or Coragen.

Cabbage Cultivation

Brassica oleracea var. capitata · Compact head formation & nutrition

Pests & Diagnostics

Black Rot (Xanthomonas) Bacterial

V-shaped chlorotic to necrotic yellow lesions on leaf margins with black veins.

Recommended Remedy: Spray Blue Copper combined with Streptocycline.
