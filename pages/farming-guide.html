Banana Cultivation

Musa acuminata · High-yield fruit crop requiring regular nutrition and fungal care

Common Diseases & Solutions

Sigatoka Leaf Spot

Yellow-brown oblong spots appear on leaves that later turn black and cause leaf drying. Reduces bunch yield severely.

Treatment: Spray Nativo Fungicide or Amistar Top at 1 ml/L.

Panama Wilt (Fusarium)

Leaves turn bright yellow along the margins and collapse near the petiole. Reddish-brown vascular staining inside the stem.

Treatment: Soil drenching around base with Bavistin or copper oxychloride.

Turmeric Cultivation

Curcuma longa · High-value rhizomatous spice

Common Diseases & Solutions

Rhizome Rot (Pythium aphanidermatum)

Basal leaves wilt and collapse. Rhizomes turn soft and watery with a foul odor during waterlogging.

Treatment: Soil drenching with Ridomil Gold (2 g/L) or Blue Copper.

Leaf Spot (Colletotrichum capsici)

Elliptical brown spots with grey centers on leaves that dry up prematurely.

Treatment: Spray Saaf Fungicide at 2 g/L.

Tomato Cultivation

Solanum lycopersicum · Continuous fruiting vegetable crop

Common Diseases & Solutions

Early and Late Blight

Dark concentric target-board spots on foliage, leading to fruit rot during cold or humid monsoon periods.

Treatment: Spray Kavach or Cabrio Top at 2 g/L.

Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera)

Caterpillars bore into green and ripening tomato fruits, causing rot and crop rejection.

Treatment: Apply Coragen Insecticide or Exponus.

Rice / Paddy Cultivation

Oryza sativa · Staple wetland cereal

Common Diseases & Solutions

Yellow Stem Borer

Larvae bore into the central stem producing 'dead hearts' at tillering and 'white ears' at heading stage.

Treatment: Spray Coragen Insecticide or Phoskill.

Paddy Blast

Spindle-shaped diamond lesions on leaves with neck rot during flowering.

Treatment: Spray Nativo Fungicide at 0.8 g/L.

Cotton Cultivation

Gossypium hirsutum · Major cash crop

Common Diseases & Solutions

Bollworm Complex

Caterpillars feed on floral squares and bore into developing bolls, causing boll drop and stained lint.

Treatment: Apply Ampligo Insecticide or Solomon.

Whitefly & Sucking Pests

Leaves curl and yellow with honey-dew secretions. Vectors cotton leaf curl virus.

Treatment: Spray Pegasus or Actara.

Cauliflower Cultivation

Brassica oleracea · Cole crop

Common Diseases & Solutions

Diamondback Moth (DBM)

Small green larvae scrape leaf tissues and bore into emerging curds.

Treatment: Apply Exponus or Coragen.

Cabbage Cultivation

Brassica oleracea · Head cabbage

Common Diseases & Solutions

Black Rot

V-shaped chlorotic lesions with darkened veins on outer leaves.

Treatment: Spray Blue Copper with bactericide.
