Broad-spectrum systemic fungicide for effective disease control

About Product

  • Jump is a premium systemic fungicide containing Trifloxystrobin 25% + Tebuconazole 50% WG
  • It provides excellent control against a wide range of fungal diseases in various crops
  • Highly effective against rust, powdery mildew, and leaf spot diseases
  • Long-lasting protection with both preventive and curative action
  • Dual mode of action reduces the risk of fungal resistance development

Technical Details

  • Active Ingredients: Trifloxystrobin 25% + Tebuconazole 50% WG
  • Mode of Action: QoI fungicide (strobilurin) + DMI fungicide (triazole)
  • Formulation: Water Dispersible Granules (WG)
  • Shelf Life: 2 years from date of manufacture
  • PHI (Pre-Harvest Interval): 14-21 days depending on crop
  • REI (Re-Entry Interval): 24 hours after application

Features

  • Dual action with protective and curative properties
  • Rain-fast within 1 hour of application
  • Excellent crop safety when used as directed
  • Compatible with most commonly used pesticides and fertilizers
  • Effectively controls resistant fungal strains
  • Improves overall plant health and crop quality

Additional Information

  • Application Rate: 0.4-0.5 g per liter of water
  • For best results, apply at first sign of disease or as a preventive measure
  • Store in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
  • Can be used in rotation with other fungicides to manage resistance
  • Provides protection for up to 21 days depending on disease pressure
  • Follow all safety precautions mentioned on the label during handling and application
