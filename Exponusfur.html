Product Description

About Product

  • Exponus is a premium broad-spectrum insecticide developed by BASE F for superior control of difficult-to-manage pests
  • Formulated with innovative active ingredients for enhanced protection and reduced resistance development
  • Provides excellent control against lepidopteran pests (caterpillars), coleopteran pests (beetles), and various sucking pests
  • Environmentally responsible formulation with minimal impact on beneficial insects and pollinators when used as directed
  • Ideal for Integrated Pest Management (IPM) programs due to its selective action

Technical Details

  • Active Ingredient: Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG
  • Formulation Type: Soluble Granules
  • Mode of Action: Primarily acts on the nervous system of target pests
  • IRAC Group: 6 (Glutamate-gated chloride channel allosteric modulators)
  • Application Rate: 0.5-0.8 g per liter of water (crop dependent)
  • Re-entry Interval: 12 hours after application
  • Pre-harvest Interval: 7-14 days depending on crop type
  • Shelf Life: 3 years when stored properly

Features

  • Dual action through contact and ingestion for comprehensive pest control
  • Rapid knockdown with visible results within 24 hours of application
  • Extended residual activity provides protection for 10-14 days
  • Translaminar movement within plant tissues for improved efficacy
  • Excellent rainfastness (resistant to wash-off) after 3 hours of application
  • Low application rates make it economical despite premium pricing
  • Compatible with most commonly used pesticides and fertilizers
  • Low odor formulation for improved applicator comfort

Additional Information

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat
  • Keep in original packaging with label information intact
  • Wear appropriate protective equipment including gloves, mask, and eye protection during handling and application
  • Use calibrated spraying equipment for accurate dosage application
  • For best results, apply during early morning or late evening to avoid hot temperatures
  • Avoid application when heavy rain is expected within 4 hours
  • Do not mix with strongly alkaline products as it may reduce efficacy
  • Always conduct a small compatibility test before tank mixing with other products
  • For technical guidance and support, contact BASE F customer service at the number provided on the package
