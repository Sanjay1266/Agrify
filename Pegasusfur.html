Premium Fungicide for Superior Disease Control

About Product

  • Pegasus is a broad-spectrum fungicide manufactured by Syngenta
  • Provides excellent protection against powdery mildew, rust, and other fungal diseases
  • Offers long-lasting protection with preventive and curative actions
  • Safe for use on various crops including vegetables, fruits, and ornamentals
  • Protects plant foliage and improves overall crop health and yield

Technical Details

  • Active Ingredient: Difenoconazole 25% EC
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable Concentrate
  • Mode of Action: Systemic with protective and curative properties
  • Target: Inhibits ergosterol biosynthesis in fungal cell membranes
  • Application Rate: 0.5-1.0 ml per liter of water (varies by crop)
  • Pre-Harvest Interval: 15-30 days (crop dependent)

Features

  • Rapid absorption and translocation throughout the plant tissues
  • Rain-fast within 2 hours of application
  • Low risk of resistance development
  • Compatible with most pesticides and fertilizers
  • Extended protection period reduces number of spray applications
  • Improves marketable yield and crop quality

Additional Information

  • Best applied early morning or late evening for optimal results
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
  • Shake well before use
  • Use appropriate personal protective equipment during application
  • For maximum effectiveness, apply before disease symptoms appear
  • Avoid application during high temperatures or when rain is expected within 2 hours
