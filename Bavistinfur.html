About Product

  • Bavistin is a premium insecticide containing 18.5% Chlorantraniliprole as its active ingredient
  • It provides long-lasting protection against a wide range of lepidopteran pests
  • Highly effective against fruit borer, stem borer, and leaf folder insects
  • Environment-friendly formulation with minimal impact on beneficial insects
  • Offers both contact and stomach action for comprehensive pest control

Technical Details

  • Active Ingredient: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC
  • Mode of Action: Ryanodine receptor modulator (Group 28 insecticide)
  • Formulation: Suspension Concentrate
  • Shelf Life: 2 years from date of manufacture
  • PHI (Pre-Harvest Interval): 7-21 days depending on crop
  • REI (Re-Entry Interval): 24 hours after application

Features

  • Provides rapid feeding cessation in target pests within hours of exposure
  • Rain-fast within 2 hours of application for reliable performance
  • Low toxicity to mammals, birds, and beneficial insects including pollinators
  • Compatible with IPM (Integrated Pest Management) practices
  • Excellent crop safety with no phytotoxicity when used as directed
  • Flexible application timing for optimal pest management
