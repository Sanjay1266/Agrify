Professional-Grade Insecticide for Comprehensive Pest Control

About Product

  • Phoskill is a professional-grade insecticide from UPL designed to control a wide range of sucking pests and chewing insects
  • Contains advanced active ingredients that provide rapid knockdown and long-lasting protection
  • Effective against aphids, thrips, whiteflies, and several other common agricultural pests
  • Safe when used as directed with low toxicity to beneficial insects when applied correctly
  • Available in multiple convenient sizes to suit different farm requirements

Technical Details

  • Active Ingredient: Chlorpyrifos 20% EC
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable Concentrate
  • Mode of Action: Contact and stomach poison
  • IRAC Group: 1B (Acetylcholinesterase inhibitor)
  • Application Rate: 1-1.5 ml per liter of water (varies by crop)
  • Re-entry Period: 24 hours after application
  • Pre-harvest Interval: 15-21 days depending on crop
  • Shelf Life: 2 years when stored in cool, dry conditions

Features

  • Broad-spectrum activity against multiple pest species
  • Quick action with visible results within 24-48 hours
  • Systemic properties allow protection of new growth
  • Rain-fast within 2-3 hours of application
  • Compatible with most other agricultural chemicals
  • Economical pest management solution for various crops
  • Minimal phytotoxicity when used as directed

Additional Information

  • Store in original container in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
  • Keep out of reach of children and away from food and animal feed
  • Use appropriate personal protective equipment during handling and application
  • Follow recommended dosage and application techniques for optimal results
  • Compatible with most fertilizers and pesticides, but jar test recommended before mixing
  • For best results, apply during early morning or late evening hours
  • For technical support, contact UPL customer service at the number provided on package
