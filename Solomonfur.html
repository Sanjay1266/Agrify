Product Description

About Product

  • Solomon is a broad-spectrum systemic fungicide developed by Bayer
  • Contains Tebuconazole 25.9% EC (Emulsifiable Concentrate)
  • Effectively controls major fungal diseases in various crops
  • Provides both preventive and curative action against fungal pathogens
  • Long-lasting protection for improved crop yield and quality

Technical Details

  • Active Ingredient: Tebuconazole 25.9% EC
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable Concentrate
  • Mode of Action: Systemic (DMI-Demethylation Inhibitor)
  • FRAC Group: 3
  • Pre-Harvest Interval: 35-45 days (crop dependent)
  • Re-Entry Period: 24 hours after application

Features

  • Systemic activity with fast uptake by plant tissues
  • Broad-spectrum control of powdery mildew, rust, leaf spots, and blights
  • Rain-fast within 2-3 hours of application
  • Compatible with most commonly used pesticides
  • Promotes overall plant health and improves yield quality
  • Prevents spore germination and inhibits mycelial growth

Crops it can be used on

Crop Target Pest
Wheat Leaf rust, Stem rust, Powdery mildew
Rice Sheath blight, Blast
Grapes Powdery mildew, Downy mildew
Mango Anthracnose, Powdery mildew
Tomato Early blight, Late blight
Chili Die-back, Fruit rot, Powdery mildew
Apple Scab, Powdery mildew
Groundnut Leaf spot, Rust

Additional Information

  • Recommended Dosage: 0.75-1.0 ml per liter of water
  • Best applied early in the disease cycle for optimal results
  • Use protective equipment (gloves, mask) during preparation and application
  • Shake well before use and mix thoroughly with water
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat
  • Keep out of reach of children and away from food and feed
  • Do not mix with alkaline pesticides
