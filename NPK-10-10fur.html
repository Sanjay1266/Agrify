Balanced Nutrition for Optimal Plant Growth

About Product

  • NPK 10-10-10 is a balanced fertilizer containing equal proportions of Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium
  • Provides essential nutrients for overall plant growth, root development, and flowering
  • Suitable for a wide range of crops including vegetables, fruits, and ornamental plants
  • Improves soil fertility and enhances crop yield
  • Easy to apply and dissolves quickly for immediate nutrient availability

Technical Details

  • Nutrient Composition: 10% Nitrogen (N), 10% Phosphorus (P₂O₅), 10% Potassium (K₂O)
  • Form: Granular
  • Solubility: Highly soluble in water
  • pH Value: 6.5-7.0
  • Shelf Life: 3 years from date of manufacture when stored properly

Features

  • Balanced NPK ratio for overall plant development
  • Promotes vegetative growth, flowering, and fruiting
  • Enhances root development and strengthens plant structure
  • Improves plant resistance to diseases and environmental stress
  • Can be used for basal application or top dressing
