Complete balanced fertilizer for optimal plant growth

About Product

  • Katanaya NPK is a premium balanced fertilizer with optimal nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium ratio (19:19:19)
  • Complete plant nutrition solution that promotes healthy growth at all stages of plant development
  • 100% water-soluble formulation for quick nutrient availability and absorption
  • Enhanced with trace elements for comprehensive plant health
  • Suitable for all soil types and can be used for both foliar application and fertigation

Technical Details

  • NPK Content: 19:19:19 (Nitrogen 19%, Phosphorus 19%, Potassium 19%)
  • Formulation: Water-soluble powder
  • pH Value: 6.5-7.2 (Neutral)
  • Micronutrients: Contains Zinc, Boron, Manganese, Copper, and Iron
  • Solubility: Over 99% soluble in water at normal temperature
  • Shelf Life: 3 years when stored properly in original packaging

Features

  • Balanced nutrition promotes vegetative growth, flowering, and fruiting
  • Enhances root development and strengthens plant structure
  • Improves photosynthesis efficiency and chlorophyll production
  • Increases resistance to environmental stress and diseases
  • Accelerates metabolic activities for higher yield potential
  • Reduces nutrient deficiency symptoms and improves crop quality

Additional Information

  • Application Rate: 2-3 g per liter of water for foliar spray; 5-10 kg per acre for soil application
  • Recommended Application Timing: Early morning or late evening for best results
  • Compatibility: Can be mixed with most pesticides and other water-soluble fertilizers
  • Storage: Keep in cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
  • Precautions: Avoid contact with eyes and prolonged skin exposure; wash hands after use
  • Best used at critical growth stages: vegetative growth, pre-flowering, and fruit development
